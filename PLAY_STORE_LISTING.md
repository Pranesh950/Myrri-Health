---

**Myrri is your private AI health coach. It turns your health data into a daily morning brief, WHOOP-style readiness and strain scores, sleep insights, a biological age estimate, and an AI coach that actually knows your body — all without a subscription, account, or ads.**

Are you tired of health and fitness apps that lock your data behind $10–30/month paywalls, sell your information, or require yet another account? Myrri is the opposite. It is a free health tracker and personal coach that runs mostly on your phone, works with the wearable you already own, and keeps your data where it belongs — with you.

---

**🤖 AI Health Coach**

Chat with a real AI coach that knows your health and food data. It reads your journal, answers questions about your sleep and readiness, logs meals for you, searches a food database, and updates your plan. Choose how it runs:

- **Fully on-device AI** — your prompts never leave your phone.
- **Bring your own key** — connect OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter.
- **No AI at all** — every core feature works completely offline.

**🩺 Readiness & Strain Scores**

Get WHOOP-style readiness and strain scores built from your own 21-day baseline of heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, and sleep — not population averages. See a daily strain target and compare the last 30 days of activity against the previous 30.

**😴 Sleep Tracking**

See how long and how well you sleep, with a sleep score based on duration, deep sleep, REM, and awake time. Understand your trends over time instead of guessing.

**📓 Habit & Journal Tracking**

Track 40+ habits in one tap — water, steps, protein, veggies, alcohol, caffeine, mood, meditation, stretching, reading, sunlight, early bedtime, and more. Build streaks, review your weekly calendar, and see which habits actually correlate with your readiness.

**🍎 Food Logging & Barcode Scanner**

- **300,000+ foods** in an offline nutrition database — log meals with no internet.
- **Barcode scanning** — point your camera at most packaged products for instant calorie, protein, carb, and fat logging; unknown barcodes fall back to AI or manual search.
- **AI food chat** — describe a meal in plain words ("two eggs, toast, coffee") and it's logged for you.
- Daily nutrition goals for calories, protein, carbs, fat, and fiber.

**🧬 Biological Age Estimate**

A wellness-oriented estimate from VO₂ max, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, movement, and body composition — anchored on published cardiorespiratory reference tables, with an honest confidence level shown alongside.

**📬 Morning Brief**

A notification each morning with yesterday's recap — goals hit, steps, sleep, calories, protein — and today's reminders. Generated on your device; works with no AI and no cloud.

**⌚ Works With Your Wearable**

Syncs with Google Health Connect (steps, heart rate, HRV, sleep, calories, VO₂ max, blood pressure, and more) and Apple Health on iOS. Works with Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung, Pixel Watch, Oura, Whoop, and more — and works fine with no wearable at all through manual journaling.

---

**🔒 Privacy by Design**

- **No account. No signup. No servers. No analytics. No ads.**
- All health data, journal entries, meals, and plans stay in local storage on your device.
- Cloud AI is optional, clearly labeled, and scrubbed of personal identifiers before anything leaves your phone. A fully on-device AI mode exists.
- We make zero money from your data — ever.

- People who want a WHOOP-style readiness and strain score without the WHOOP subscription.
- Anyone who wants an AI health coach without paying monthly or selling their data.
- Wearable owners (Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung, Pixel Watch, Oura, Whoop) who want their data analyzed on their own phone.
- Anyone who wants better sleep, habits, and nutrition — with or without a wearable.

## 4. Play Console reminders

- **App category:** Health & Fitness
- **App type:** Application
- **Content rating questionnaire:** health/fitness data → complete honestly; the app collects health data but stores it locally (no servers)
- **Data safety form:** declare *health and fitness data* — **not collected** if it never leaves the device, but check the "processed ephemerally" options if any AI provider receives scrubbed data
- **Health Connect API Access declaration:** required by Google Play for any app that reads Health Connect data (this app requests steps, heart rate, sleep, HRV, VO₂ max, blood pressure, exercise sessions, and more) — complete this form in Play Console before rollout
- **Privacy policy URL:** host the `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` content (required by Play)
- **Store listing experiments (CSLs):** A/B test the short description alternates after launch — Google feeds conversion rate back into ranking
- **Android Vitals:** keep crash-free sessions high — stability directly affects Play ranking
